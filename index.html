Welcome to the Dyad Gong Timer

This app conducts 38-minute dyads consisting of 8 cycles.

About

  • After each cycle, 3 gongs play
  • At the end of the dyad, 5 gongs play in quick succession

Controls

  • Tap the center of the dial to pause / play
  • Drag around the dial ring to change the current time
  • The bottom buttons skip ±30s, jump to the quarter points (• •• •••), or skip to just before the next gong (>>)

Tips

  • Turn your volume up — use the test gong on the title screen
  • Keep this page open during the session
