Welcome to the Dyad Gong Timer

This app conducts a 38-minute dyad made up of 8 cycles.

  • After each cycle, 3 gongs play
  • At the end of the dyad, 5 gongs play in quick succession

Main controls

  • Tap the center of the dial to pause / play
  • Drag around the ring to change the time

Buttons

  • −30s / +30s skip back or forward
  • •  ••  ••• jump to the quarter points
  • >> skips to just before the next gong

Before you start

  • Turn your volume up — use the test gong on the title screen
  • Keep this page open during the session

Add to Home Screen

For a full-screen timer without the browser bars, add this app to your Home Screen and open it from there.

  • Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow) at the bottom of Safari
  • Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add
  • Tap the browser menu (⋮) in the top corner
  • Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app), then confirm
